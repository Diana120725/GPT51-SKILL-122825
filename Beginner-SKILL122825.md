GETTING STARTED
What is SKILLS.md?
SKILLS.md is a comprehensive reference document that describes 20 specialized AI capabilities. Think of it as a "recipe book" for AI tasks - each skill is a detailed recipe you can follow to accomplish specific professional tasks.
Who Should Use This Guide?

✓ Business professionals new to AI tools
✓ Students learning AI-assisted work
✓ Managers wanting to leverage AI
✓ Anyone who wants to automate documentation and analysis tasks

Prerequisites
No technical experience required! You only need:

Access to an AI platform (Claude, ChatGPT, etc.)
Documents or data you want to work with
Clear understanding of what you want to accomplish

**Regression Equation:**

**VIF Values:** All below 3.0, indicating no multicollinearity concerns

### 5.3 Key Insights from Regression

**Most Important Drivers (in order):**
1. **Management Quality** (β = 0.45)
   - For every 1-point improvement in management rating, satisfaction 
     increases by 0.45 points (holding other factors constant)
   
2. **Work-Life Balance** (β = 0.28)
   - Second most important factor
   - Nearly 2x more important than compensation

3. **Recognition** (β = 0.18)
   - Feeling valued/recognized significantly impacts satisfaction

**Surprising Finding:**
Compensation and Benefits, while statistically significant, have 
relatively small impact compared to management and work-life balance. 
This suggests that increasing pay alone won't dramatically improve 
satisfaction without addressing other factors.

**Practical Implication:**
A 1-point improvement in management rating has the same impact on 
satisfaction as a 5.6-point improvement in compensation rating. 
This strongly suggests that **investing in management development may 
be more effective than compensation increases** for improving overall 
employee satisfaction.
